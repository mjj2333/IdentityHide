## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is a face blur app?
A face blur app lets you obscure recognizable faces in photos before saving or sharing them. Redact.ID includes automatic face detection as well as manual and paint-on blur tools.
### Can Redact.ID automatically blur faces?
Yes. Redact.ID can automatically detect faces in a photo so you can quickly apply a blur without manually locating every face.
### Can I manually blur a face?
Yes. Redact.ID includes manual blur controls when you want to choose exactly what gets obscured.
### Can I paint blur onto part of a photo?
Yes. Redact.ID’s paint-on blur gives you more precise control by allowing you to apply blur directly to the areas you want hidden.
### Can Redact.ID detect multiple faces?
Yes. Automatic face detection can identify multiple faces within an image. You can then review the image and make additional manual adjustments if necessary.
### Does Redact.ID store my photos?
Redact.ID is designed so your photos and edits are stored locally on your device rather than being maintained in an online photo library on Redact.ID’s servers.
Some features that require additional processing, such as AI tattoo removal, require an image to be sent for GPU processing.
### Does Redact.ID remove EXIF metadata?
Yes. Redact.ID includes EXIF metadata removal as another way to reduce information contained within an image file before you share it.
### Does blurring my face make me anonymous?
Not necessarily. A face is only one piece of information that could identify someone in a photograph. Tattoos, backgrounds, text, reflections, clothing and other details may still reveal information about you.
For a more complete approach, read our guide to **[protecting your identity in photos before posting online](/post-photos-anonymously)**.
