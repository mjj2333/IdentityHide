## Why Remove Tattoos From Photos?
There are plenty of reasons someone might want a tattoo removed from an image.
Maybe you simply don’t want it in a particular photo.
Maybe you’re creating content and want a cleaner image.
Or maybe the tattoo itself could reveal your identity.
### Tattoos Can Be Identifying
A unique tattoo can act as a recognizable physical characteristic.
If the same tattoo appears on your public social media account and an account you intend to keep anonymous, it may provide another visual detail that could connect those photos.
This can be particularly relevant for content creators, online sex workers, models and anyone maintaining separation between different online identities.
Covering your face doesn’t necessarily hide everything that makes you recognizable.
Removing distinctive tattoos can help reduce another source of identifying information in a photo.

Before posting an image anonymously, look at the entire photo rather than focusing only on your face.
